Accessibility at Respiratory Basics

Last reviewed: November 19, 2026

Respiratory Basics should work for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, voice control, magnification or larger text. Our goal is to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA on this website, in the web app and in the phone apps.

What we have done

  • Every page has a "Skip to content" link, a clear heading structure and labeled navigation.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, including the practice questions on this site, and keyboard focus is always visible.
  • Images have text alternatives; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and buttons are checked for color contrast, and right and wrong answers are marked with a symbol and words, not color alone.
  • In the apps, answer choices and buttons have screen reader labels for VoiceOver and TalkBack, and text grows with your device's text size setting.

How we check

We run automated accessibility checks (axe) on the pages of this site and in the web app, and we test with a keyboard and a screen reader.

Known limits

  • Some study material is visual by nature, such as ECG strips and other figures. Where we can, the question, rationale or a caption describes what the figure shows, but strip-reading drills ask you to read the picture itself.

Tell us about a barrier

If anything on this site or in the apps is hard to use with your setup, please email contact@rnbasics.com with the page or screen and what happened. We read every message, aim to reply within a few business days, and will get you the content in a form that works for you while we fix it.
